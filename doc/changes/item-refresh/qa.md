## Exploratory session

A throwaway Playwright script drove the running app. It ran from a temporary directory with a separate config, and the directory was deleted afterwards. The randomized part used a seeded LCG (`seed = 20261003`, `seed = (seed*1103515245+12345) mod 2^31`) to pick from `tabAmazon, tabLbc, refreshRandom, openCloseMenu, refreshAll, wait` for 15 steps. It started at the Amazon tab, 1280x800, right after QA-008. The recorded order was:

0 refreshAll (202) · 1 openCloseMenu Kindle (Refresh disabled, pending) · 2 refreshRandom Sony (disabled, not clicked) · 3 refreshAll (button disabled, no-op) · 4 refreshRandom Nike (disabled) · 5 tabLbc · 6 wait · 7 tabLbc · 8 refreshRandom Bestway (disabled) · 9 refreshRandom Bestway (disabled) · 10 wait · 11 openCloseMenu Bestway (enabled after refresh-all finished) · 12 tabAmazon · 13 wait · 14 wait.

Outcome: no page errors and no leftover notifications. Every row ended in its server-reported final status. The menu's disabled state matched the pending state at every step. Replay with the same seed is deterministic, but the timing of real collection is not.

## Coverage and limitations

- Real marketplaces returned failures (Amazon 404, LeBoncoin 403) at collection time. A live successful price update after a single refresh could not be observed. Only the failure path (status changes, last price retained) was observed live. The success path is covered by the mocked automated test (QA-011).
- "Remains available during refresh-all" could not be exercised live, because the backend marks every item pending during a bulk refresh. The disabled behavior for pending items was verified.
- Observation (pre-existing, not introduced by this change, not a requirement failure): the row menu trigger's accessible name is "Options". The `aria-label="Actions for <item>"` on `OverflowMenu` does not reach the button that screen readers and Playwright see. FR-008 refers to the "existing labelled 'Actions for <item>' menu". The existing automated tests also locate the trigger by "Options". Referred to the coordinator/adjudicator as QA-OBS-001 for a decision. It does not block keyboard operation.
- `go test`/`go vet` were not re-run in this QA stage (no backend changes).
- No independent probe of ports 5173/3001 after shutdown was possible, because tool permissions blocked curl/ps. See the cleanup notes below.
